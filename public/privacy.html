Privacy

Honeydle has no cookies, ads or trackers, and never asks for your name or email.

Your browser keeps a random player ID so your streak and progress stick around. Our server stores that ID with your answers and scores to run the game and the daily stats.

We don't sell or share any of it. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs.

To start fresh, clear this site's data in your browser.
