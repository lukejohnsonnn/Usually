Honeydle: a free daily guessing game built on real numbers

Every day, everyone gets the same seven prompts. Every answer is a real published statistic from a survey, census, government figure or study, so the game is about reasoning your way to a real number, not trivia recall.

How to play

  • Guess the %: slide or type your estimate on a 100-cell honeycomb. The closer you are, the more points.
  • Name the #1: type an answer from a ranked list. The higher it ranks, the more points.
  • Beat the clock: 15 seconds to guess, 25 to type.
  • Fill your honey jar, share your score, and come back tomorrow for a new Daily. Endless practice is always open.

Topics include history, science, space, business, money, pop culture, geography and the human body. Honeydle is free and needs no account.
